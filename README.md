# Monthly Sales Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **monthly sales performance over a 24-month period (January 2024 – December 2025)** to evaluate revenue trends, order volume, items sold, Average Order Value (AOV), and product performance.

The analysis focuses on identifying **sales growth patterns, high- and low-performing months, and top-performing product categories and brands** to support strategic sales and merchandising decisions.

The analysis was conducted using **Google BigQuery (GoogleSQL)** with CTEs, date functions, window functions, conditional filtering, and multi-level aggregation.

---

## 🗂️ Dataset

The analysis uses the **TheLook Ecommerce** public dataset in Google BigQuery.

The analysis combines data from three tables:

- `orders` — order-level transaction information
- `order_items` — item-level transaction and sales information
- `products` — product category, brand, and product attributes

### Columns Primarily Used in the Analysis

The analysis primarily uses the following columns from the three source tables:

#### `orders`
- `created_at` — defines the monthly analysis period
- `order_id` — calculates the number of unique orders
- `num_of_item` — calculates total items sold
- `status` — filters completed orders

#### `order_items`
- `order_id` — joins order-level data with item-level data
- `sale_price` — calculates monthly revenue and AOV
- `product_id` — connects transactions with product attributes
- `status` — filters completed order items for product-level analysis

#### `products`
- `id` — joins product information with order items
- `category` — analyzes revenue and order performance by category
- `brand` — analyzes revenue and order performance by brand

This relationship allows order-level metrics to be combined with item-level sales and product attributes.

## 📊 Key Metrics

### Monthly Revenue

Total sales revenue generated from completed orders during each month.

### Total Orders

Number of unique completed orders during each month.

### Total Items Sold

Total number of items included in completed orders.

### Average Order Value (AOV)

Average revenue generated per completed order.

### Revenue MoM Growth

Percentage change in monthly revenue compared with the previous month.

### AOV MoM Growth

Percentage change in monthly AOV compared with the previous month.

### Brand Revenue

Total revenue generated by each brand during the analysis period.

### Brand Order Volume

Number of unique orders associated with each brand.

## 📈 Insight

### 🚀 Strong Revenue Growth

Revenue increased by **130.4%** over the 24-month analysis period, growing from **$30,393 in January 2024 to $70,036 in December 2025**.

**December 2025** recorded the highest monthly revenue during the analysis period.

This indicates a strong overall upward sales trend despite month-to-month fluctuations.

---

### 📈 Revenue Growth Volatility in 2024

The strongest monthly revenue growth occurred in **July 2024**, with **+24.7% MoM growth**.

The largest monthly decline occurred in **June 2024**, with **-12.66% MoM growth**.

This indicates notable sales volatility during the middle of 2024.

---

### 🛒 Order Volume Increased Significantly

Monthly order volume increased by approximately **119%**, from **357 orders in January 2024 to 782 orders in December 2025**.

Items sold also more than doubled, increasing from **959 to 2,150 items** over the same period.

The simultaneous increase in orders and items sold indicates that **transaction volume was a major contributor to revenue growth**.

---

### 💰 Revenue Growth Was Primarily Volume-Driven

AOV remained relatively stable at approximately **$81–$94** throughout the analysis period.

This suggests that revenue growth was driven primarily by **higher order volume rather than a substantial increase in average order value**.

Therefore, maintaining customer acquisition and purchase frequency is an important driver of continued revenue growth.

---

### 📊 AOV Volatility

The strongest AOV increase occurred in **July 2025**, with **+12.24% MoM growth**.

The largest AOV decline occurred in **March 2025**, at **-11.92% MoM growth**.

Although AOV fluctuated between months, the overall range remained relatively stable compared with the substantial increase in order volume.

---

### 👖 Top-Performing Categories & Brands

**Jeans** and **Outerwear & Coats** were among the strongest product categories based on brand-level revenue.

The top-performing brands identified in the analysis were:

| Rank | Brand | Category | Revenue |
|---:|---|---|---:|
| 1 | 7 For All Mankind | Jeans | $13,622 |
| 2 | Carhartt | Outerwear & Coats | $12,533 |
| 3 | Joe's Jeans | Jeans | $10,852 |

**7 For All Mankind** generated the highest revenue among the analyzed brands, followed by **Carhartt** and **Joe's Jeans**.

---

### 🏊 High-Volume, Lower-Ticket Brand

**Speedo** recorded the highest order volume with **137 orders**.

Despite generating lower revenue per item than some of the top-revenue brands, its high order volume indicates a **high-frequency, lower-ticket purchase pattern**.

This demonstrates why brand performance should be evaluated using both **revenue and order volume**, rather than revenue alone.

## 🧮 SQL Techniques Demonstrated

This project demonstrates practical SQL techniques commonly used in Data Analyst workflows.

### CTE

CTEs were used to organize monthly sales and AOV calculations into logical analytical steps.

```sql
WITH base AS (
    SELECT
        DATE_TRUNC(o.created_at, MONTH) AS bulan_tahun,
        COUNT(DISTINCT o.order_id) AS total_order,
        SUM(o.num_of_item) AS total_item,
        SUM(oi.sale_price) AS revenue_month
    FROM `bigquery-public-data.thelook_ecommerce.orders` o
    JOIN `bigquery-public-data.thelook_ecommerce.order_items` oi
        USING(order_id)
    WHERE o.status = 'Complete'
    GROUP BY bulan_tahun
)
```

### Date Functions

`DATE_TRUNC()` was used to aggregate transactions into monthly periods.

```sql
DATE_TRUNC(o.created_at, MONTH)
```

### COUNT DISTINCT

`COUNT(DISTINCT order_id)` was used to calculate unique order volume.

```sql
COUNT(DISTINCT o.order_id)
```

### Multi-Level Aggregation

The category and brand analysis aggregates transaction data by product category and brand.

```sql
GROUP BY
    p.category,
    p.brand
```

### Window Functions

`LAG()` was used to compare the current month's revenue or AOV with the previous month.

```sql
LAG(revenue_month) OVER (
    ORDER BY bulan_tahun
)
```

This enables calculation of Month-over-Month growth.

### Conditional Filtering

Completed transactions were selected using order and order-item status conditions.

```sql
WHERE o.status = 'Complete'
  AND oi.status = 'Complete'
```
